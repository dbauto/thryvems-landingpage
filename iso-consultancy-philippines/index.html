ISO consultant and consultancy services in the Philippines

Thync & Thryve provides ISO training and consultancy for organizations building or improving management systems. We work with your team on requirements, process documentation, implementation and audit readiness, with the scope agreed around your operations and the standard you need.

ISO consulting services built around your operations

Our approach connects the selected ISO requirements to the work your people actually do. An engagement starts by understanding your products or services, sites, customer commitments and existing processes. We then agree the support your team needs, who owns each activity and which outputs belong in the proposal.

You can discuss a focused requirement or a broader implementation programme. Training and consultancy are scoped to your organization; certification-body audits and fees remain separate. Use the service stages below to prepare a useful brief.

  1. Gap assessment and project planning
  2. Process documentation and document control
  3. ISO implementation support
  4. Requirements and internal auditor training
  5. Internal audit and certification audit preparation

ISO gap assessment and project planning

Start with a clear scope: the activities, sites and standard your management system will cover. Gap-assessment work compares existing practices and evidence with relevant requirements, so priorities reflect your starting point.

Discuss a gap-assessment report and an action plan that identifies owners, priorities and evidence to gather. Confirm the assessment method and agreed outputs in your proposal before work begins. This gives management a basis for planning resources and realistic milestones.

ISO documentation support

We help teams connect process descriptions, responsibilities and records to their actual operations. Documentation support can address process mapping, procedures, document control, risk records and the evidence needed to show controls are used.

Agree who drafts, reviews and approves each work product. Your people should be able to use the resulting instructions and find current records; a collection of generic templates cannot demonstrate that a management system works.

ISO implementation and management system improvement

Implementation turns an action plan into day-to-day practice. Our consultancy approach works with process owners to understand requirements, assign responsibilities, introduce controls and review evidence of their use. Your organization remains accountable for operating and improving its system.

For quality management, explore our ISO 9001 certification preparation and QMS consultancy guide. If multiple standards apply, discuss which processes can be shared in an integrated management system and which requirements need separate controls.

ISO training and internal auditor preparation

Training helps staff understand requirements, their responsibilities and the evidence their work creates. Discuss whether your team needs awareness sessions, requirements interpretation, process-owner workshops or internal auditor training.

Our ISO internal auditor training guide explains planning, evidence gathering, findings and follow-up. Confirm the selected standard, participants, delivery arrangements, dates and fees for the proposed engagement.

Internal audit support and certification audit preparation

Audit readiness depends on operating evidence: implemented controls, internal audit findings, corrective actions and management review. Discuss the internal-audit programme, the competence and objectivity of the people involved, and how findings will be followed through.

Certification audit preparation helps your team organize evidence and understand the assessment stages. The independent certification body conducts its own audit and decides whether certification can be granted. Our guide to getting ISO certified in the Philippines explains that process.

What to ask an ISO consultant to deliver

A useful proposal identifies the standard, covered sites, responsibilities, deliverables and exclusions. Agree which gap-assessment, documentation, implementation, training and audit-preparation activities are included, along with review and approval responsibilities.

Consultancy fees depend on the starting point, scope and support required. Independent certification body fees, travel arrangements where relevant and ongoing surveillance should be clear. Our ISO certification cost guide for the Philippines helps you compare quotations on the same basis.

Prepare a short brief with your activities, approximate staffing, sites, existing management system and target date. Avoid sending confidential operational or customer records through a public enquiry.

Consultancy and certification are different

An ISO consultant supports preparation and improvement; an independent certification body evaluates the management system and makes the certification decision. Check the body's accreditation and scope before engaging it. Consultancy does not include a guaranteed certificate or audit result.

Software is a separate decision. ThryveMS QMS software is in development to help organize ISO 9001-related work. It does not replace management accountability or independent assessment.

ISO consultancy: frequently asked questions

What does an ISO consultant do?

An ISO consultant helps your team understand requirements, assess gaps, plan implementation, prepare documentation and build audit readiness. Your organization operates the system; an independent certification body audits it and decides whether to issue a certificate.

Which ISO standard should my business start with?

Start with customer or contractual requirements and your operational risks. ISO 9001 addresses quality management; other standards cover environment, worker safety, information security and food safety. Agree the standard and scope before planning an engagement.

What is included in ISO consultancy fees?

The agreed proposal should identify assessment, documentation support, training, implementation and audit-preparation work, with responsibilities and exclusions. Independent certification-body audit and surveillance fees should be shown separately.

How long does ISO implementation take?

The schedule depends on your starting point, sites, staffing, standard and the time needed to put controls into use and gather records. Agree milestones after an assessment; a single fixed timeline is not appropriate for every organization.

Can consultancy and training be delivered remotely?

Discuss your locations, participants and assessment needs before agreeing delivery arrangements. Some activities may suit remote workshops; site-dependent work and availability must be confirmed for your project.

Does Thync & Thryve issue ISO certificates or guarantee certification?

No. We provide training and consultancy. Independent certification bodies make certification decisions. ThryveMS is a separate QMS software product in development and does not issue certificates or guarantee an audit outcome.
