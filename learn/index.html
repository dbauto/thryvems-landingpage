ISO learning resources

Understand management systems and certification before deciding on training or consultancy.

Where to start

Start with your business activities, customers, contractual needs, sites and process owners. Different ISO standards focus on different areas such as quality, environmental performance, occupational safety, information security and food safety. Choose the subject that relates to your organization rather than assuming every business needs every standard. A useful management system should reflect actual operations and responsibilities, not just a collection of purchased templates.

How certification works

Preparing for assessment usually means defining the scope, understanding requirements, operating processes, evaluating results and addressing gaps. Internal audits, management review and corrective actions provide evidence of the system in use. Read the ISO certification process guide for practical steps. An independent certification body makes the certification decision; a consultant or software tool cannot award a certificate.

Compare costs and choose a next step

Separate consultancy and training from independent certification-body audit charges. Costs depend on scope, staffing, sites, audit effort and follow-up visits. Use the certification cost guide to compare quoted inclusions and exclusions. If staff need more knowledge, see training topics. If you need help improving processes, see consultancy services. Frequently asked questions

Which ISO standard should I learn first?

Begin with the requirements, customers and business risks relevant to the activities you perform.

Does every company need certification?

Not necessarily. Customer, contractual and legal requirements vary by organization.

Is certification the same as consultancy?

No. Only an independent certification body makes certification decisions.

Do these guides guarantee a certificate?

No. They are learning resources, not a certification process by themselves.

How can I compare quotations?

Separate consultancy, training and certification-body costs and check each offer's assumptions.
