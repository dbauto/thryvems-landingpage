Food safety management | ISO 22000

ISO 22000 consultant in the Philippines

ISO 22000 provides requirements for a food safety management system (FSMS) that applies to organizations in the food chain. It brings together communication, management responsibilities, prerequisite programmes and hazard-control activities. For a Philippine food processor, ingredient supplier or catering operation, useful implementation starts with real products, facilities, process conditions and food-safety hazards.

Understand the food chain and scope

A system may cover manufacturing, food handling, storage, distribution or another defined activity in the food chain. Identify product groups, processes, suppliers, customers, site boundaries and outsourced steps. The chosen scope must reflect the activities actually controlled by the business.

Trace a product from receipt through processing, storage and delivery. Information from procurement, maintenance, sanitation, quality and operations often reveals gaps that are not obvious from the final product specification alone.

Build prerequisite programmes around the facility

Prerequisite programmes support hygienic operating conditions and prevention of contamination. Their design may involve cleaning, pest management, water quality, waste control, personnel hygiene, equipment condition, zoning or other controls relevant to the facility. Exact requirements depend on the sector, product and applicable regulations.

Records should show that important controls are performed and verified. A checklist copied from another type of food facility may miss critical hazards or create tasks that do not fit actual operations.

Analyse hazards with a competent team

A food-safety team considers biological, chemical, physical and other relevant hazards at each process stage. It reviews the significance of those hazards and selects appropriate control measures. Decisions about operational prerequisite programmes and critical control points require a justified methodology and competent technical judgment.

Verification and validation are different: validation supports that a selected control can achieve its intended outcome, while verification checks that the system is operating as planned. Both concepts matter for a credible food-safety system.

Clarify Philippine requirements

Food businesses need to determine applicable food safety, licensing, inspection and product requirements under Philippine law. Depending on the activity, agencies, permits and industry schemes can differ. ISO 22000 certification should not be presented as a substitute for required permits, food registrations or government inspections.

Export buyers may impose additional product, customer or supply-chain requirements. Confirm those separately, including whether buyers request ISO 22000, FSSC 22000 or another scheme. They are not interchangeable terms.

Rehearse traceability and incident response

Test whether incoming materials and finished products can be identified and traced as needed. A withdrawal or recall exercise can reveal whether lot information, customer contacts and decision-making roles are usable under pressure. The exercise should lead to a documented improvement if the response is too slow or incomplete.

Discuss internal and external communication: suppliers, customers, authorities where required and teams managing production. People must know when to stop, hold, escalate or release product.

Prepare for independent assessment

Before certification, check hazard analysis decisions, prerequisite evidence, internal audits, corrective action, traceability tests and management review. Certification bodies will consider the defined food-chain scope and applicable certification requirements. Choosing an accredited and competent body matters.

A consultant can help plan and improve the FSMS, but the organization owns daily food safety. Ask for a clear project scope separating training, technical implementation, internal readiness and the independent certification audit.

Frequently asked questions

Can ISO 22000 apply to a small food business?

Yes, when an FSMS is appropriate and scaled to the activities and hazards.

Is ISO 22000 the same as HACCP?

They are related, but ISO 22000 is a broader management system with food-safety hazard control.

Does certification replace Philippine food permits?

No. Applicable regulatory obligations remain separate.

Can we claim FSSC 22000 if we have ISO 22000?

No. FSSC 22000 is a distinct scheme with additional requirements.

What evidence matters before an audit?

Relevant prerequisite controls, hazard analysis, traceability, verification, internal audits and management review.

Does the consultant issue the certificate?

No. The certification decision is made by an independent certification body.
